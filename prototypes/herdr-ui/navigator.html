    How to drive it

    Click the terminal. g (= prefix g) opens or focuses the githerd pane in the current workspace. Inside it: j/k move, l/enter/→ drill in, h/esc/← back, z zoom pane, o open repo in its workspace, s shell split, L lazygit, f fetch. Outside it: o next pane, w next workspace, z zoom, x close pane. Click sidebar rows to switch workspace.

    Do things in Herdr itself (not via githerd)

    Herdr emits workspace.created/.closed/.focused to githerd's hooks. A cd emits nothing hookable (pane.updated can't trigger hooks), so githerd's view lags until the next trigger: watch the map below.
